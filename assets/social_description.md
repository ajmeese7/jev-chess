# Social image

`social.svg` is the source, `social.png` (1280x640, rendered at `--scale 1`; Aaron found the 2x render looked jagged in his viewer) is what gets uploaded. Canvas is 1280x640, text starts at x=100 (safe zone edge) to leave a clear gap before the board, which is decoration and sits at x 800..1232.

## Concept

A board position where the white knight on d4 has its eight candidate squares heat-mapped in emerald by Jev's probability mass, with the argmax (Nxe5, the black pawn) boxed in white and labelled `0.62`. The point is "the model returns a distribution over legal moves and we play the max", which is the whole engine. Probabilities are illustrative, not from a real call.

## Text

Title "Jev Chess", 116px DejaVu Sans bold, white. Tagline on two lines, 38px, `#a1a1aa`: "No search. No eval." / "Jev picks every move." A one-line tagline at this size collides with the board; do not merge the lines.

Rejected: a `POST /v1/evaluate` mono footer (Aaron dropped it for whitespace) and the repo URL (redundant next to a link preview).

## Palette

Background `#0f0f11`, panel `#18181b`, accent `#10b981` (favicon and UI emerald), accent text `#34d399`, muted `#a1a1aa`, board squares `#3f3f46` / `#27272a`. Heat squares are the accent at `0.15 + 0.75 * p` opacity.

## Alternatives turned down

B: the app's thinking panel (SAN moves with probability bars). C: the favicon pawn scaled up with a glow. Both rendered and reviewed on 2026-09-21; A won for being the most chess-specific.

## Rendering

Portable font stack (DejaVu Sans / DejaVu Sans Mono), so no `--font-file` or font embedding is needed. Pieces are Unicode chess glyphs; black pieces are dark-filled with a `#a1a1aa` outline so the two sides differ. Regenerate with:

```sh
~/.claude/skills/social-image/convert.sh assets/social.svg --scale 1
```

The SVG was generated by a throwaway script (not committed). Editing the SVG directly is fine; the board is a flat list of `<rect>` and `<text>` elements.
